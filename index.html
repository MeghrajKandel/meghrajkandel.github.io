About Me

I am a researcher in artificial intelligence and data science, specialising in real-time tourism demand forecasting. My work is defined by building systems that are not just accurate — but adaptive.

My primary focus is the ATLAS framework (Adaptive Tourism LLM with Aligned Signals), an AI-driven system that leverages large language models and aligned data streams to improve forecasting accuracy at scale.

Currently pursuing a Master of ICT (Research) at Melbourne Institute of Technology, I bring a deep understanding of both the algorithmic and applied sides of intelligent systems.
